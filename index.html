Profile

Open to conversations

My work sits where applied AI meets engineering constraints. At Moffett Automation I lead AI-driven process automation and build machine vision pipelines for industrial robotics. Alongside that I have taken three agentic AI systems into production — document inspection and orchestration for sales and engineering, a multimodal computer vision platform for real estate, and a workforce management system running in the field.

My research background is in event-based vision. My MSc thesis at TU Dortmund built a self-supervised model for 3D prediction of logistic objects using event cameras, reaching +6.3 mAP over the baseline while cutting annotation storage by 90%.

Before AI, I certified software that cannot fail: verification and validation of DAL-A production software for the Airbus MRTT. That grounding in traceability and reliability is what I bring to machine learning systems.
