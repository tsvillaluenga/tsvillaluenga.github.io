About

Most of my work is software: Python and C++ services, vision pipelines, and LLM-based systems that have to run unattended. At Moffett Automation I work on machine vision for industrial robotics and on AI-driven process automation.

Outside that I have designed and shipped three AI systems that are in production today — an agentic document inspection and orchestration service, a multimodal computer vision platform, and a workforce management system with vision-based check-in.

My research background is event-based vision. For my MSc thesis at TU Dortmund I built a self-supervised model that predicts 3D annotations for logistic objects from event-camera data. Before moving into AI I spent a year on verification and validation of DAL-A software for the Airbus MRTT, which is where I learned how much traceability and testing discipline matter.
