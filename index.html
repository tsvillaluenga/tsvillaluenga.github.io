About me

AI, Agentic Systems & Robotics

Robotics Software Engineer at Moffett Automation.
Previously Airbus Defence and Space (DAL-A safety-critical software), Dronomy (UAV autonomy) and Santillana · Sanoma Group (AI & automation consulting). MSc Robotics & Automation (UPM · TU Dortmund). Patent holder and published author.

Agentic AI & LLM Engineering RAG & LLMOps Computer Vision Robotics & Autonomous Systems Machine Learning Deep Learning Event-based Vision Embedded & Safety-Critical Systems


I design and ship intelligent systems that merge applied AI, robotics and embedded engineering. Day to day I lead AI-driven process automation and build machine vision pipelines for industrial robotics, and I have taken several agentic AI systems into production — from document inspection and orchestration for sales and engineering teams, to multimodal computer vision platforms and workforce management software used in the field.

My research background sits in event-based vision. My MSc thesis at TU Dortmund developed a self-supervised learning algorithm for 3D prediction of logistic objects using event cameras, combining NeRF-inspired 3D modelling with efficient dataset creation to reach +6.3 mAP over the baseline while reducing annotation storage by 90%. I later completed advanced training in Deep Unsupervised Learning, Generative AI and Computer Vision at the University of Oxford, graded A+.

I also hold a patent derived from my Bachelor's Thesis and have collaborated on research projects in Spain and Germany, strengthening both my technical depth and my international perspective. I am always open to conversations around applied AI research, agentic systems and robotics.


Languages: Spanish (native), English (C1), German (A2).
